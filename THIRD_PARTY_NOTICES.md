Extracted from Niyas V's portfolio, commit `053f492`. Original portfolio images, real guest entries, configuration and notification services are excluded.

React, React DOM, Three.js, Motion, react-pageflip, Vite, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript and Vitest retain their MIT licenses. Lucide icons retain their ISC license. Full dependency notices are included in the installed packages.

See `docs/ink-sketch-prompts.json` for the subjects, prompts and saved asset paths.
